Potty Rogue Support

Stuck in the pipes? Email pottyrogue@hirehumansignal.com and we'll get back to you. Telling us your iPhone model and iOS version helps.

How do I play?

Swipe on the board to move. Moving into a baddie attacks it, and plunger hits stun. Tap an item in your bag to see what it does, then swipe to throw it or tap a glowing tile to use it. Settings has a button to show the tutorial again. For stats, drop rates, and strategy, see the Potty Rogue Wiki.

Does my run save?

Yes. Your run saves after every move, so you can close the app mid-floor and pick up later with Continue Sitting.

I bought something but don't have it

Open Extras from the title screen and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

There's no sound

Potty Rogue follows your iPhone's silent switch, and its music steps aside while other audio is playing. Music plays quietly under the sound effects. Sound effects and music each have their own switch in the pause menu and in Settings.

Game Center

Leaderboards and achievements need Game Center, which you can sign in to in the Settings app. Scores and achievements you earned while signed out are sent the next time you open the game signed in.

Can I start over?

Settings has an Erase All Progress button. It clears your Work Log, Compoopdium, Flush Coins, and Guild unlocks. Purchases are kept and can be restored.
