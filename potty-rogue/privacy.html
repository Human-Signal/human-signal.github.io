Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026

Potty Rogue is made by Human Signal LLC. The short version: we don't collect any data about you.

What we collect

Nothing. Potty Rogue has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers of ours. It doesn't use tracking of any kind.

Your saves

Your Work Log, Compoopdium, Flush Coins, unlocks, and records are stored on your device. Progress does not sync between iPhone and Android.

On iPhone, if you're signed in to iCloud, a copy is also kept in your own iCloud account so progress comes back on a new iPhone. That copy lives in your iCloud account; we can't see it.

On Android, those same files can restore through your Google account's Auto Backup. That backup is in your Google account; we can't see it.

The run in progress and your settings stay on the device you're playing on. You can erase your progress from Settings in the game (on iPhone that also clears the iCloud copy).

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple (iPhone) or Google Play (Android). We never see your payment details. The app asks the store which purchases your account owns so it can unlock them again after a reinstall.

Game Center and Play Games

If you're signed in to Game Center (iPhone) or Play Games (Android), the game sends your best score, deepest floor, and achievement progress to that service so they appear on leaderboards and in your achievements. Those services are run by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies, and you can sign out at any time in system settings.

Children

Potty Rogue doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
