Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026

Potty Rogue is made by Human Signal LLC. The short version: we don't collect any data about you.

What we collect

Nothing. Potty Rogue has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers of ours. It doesn't use tracking of any kind.

Your saves and iCloud

Your Work Log, Compoopdium, Flush Coins, unlocks, and records are stored on your device. If you're signed in to iCloud, a copy is also kept in your own iCloud account, so your progress comes back on a new iPhone and stays in step across your devices. That copy lives in your iCloud account, where only you can access it; we can't see it. The run in progress and your settings stay on the device you're playing on.

You can erase your progress from Settings in the game, which also erases the iCloud copy.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. The app asks Apple which purchases your Apple Account owns so it can unlock them.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, the game sends your best score, deepest floor, and achievement progress to Apple's Game Center so they appear on leaderboards and in your achievements. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy, and you can sign out of it in the Settings app at any time.

Children

Potty Rogue doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
