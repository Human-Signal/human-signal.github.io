Perks

Every level-up heals you to full and offers 3 different perks. You get 9 level-ups in a run (level 1 to 10), so you'll see a lot of choices but only keep 9.

How offers work

Each of the 3 slots rolls a rarity (Common 60%, Uncommon 28%, Rare 10%, Legendary 2%) and then picks evenly within it. There are no duplicates in one offer, and a perk stops showing up once you've taken it the maximum number of times. The Lucky Wipe Extra gives you one free reroll per run.
