Perks

Every level-up heals you to full and offers 3 different perks. You get 15 level-ups in a run (level 1 to 16), so you'll see a lot of choices but only keep 15.

How offers work

Each of the 3 slots rolls a rarity among those that still have a take left (Common 61%, Uncommon 28%, Rare 10%, Legendary 0.4%) and then picks evenly within it. There are no duplicates in one offer, and a perk stops showing up once you've taken it the maximum number of times. Legendary starts very rare (~10% of runs see Double Time by level 16), then shows up more once other rarities are maxed. The Lucky Wipe Extra gives you one free reroll per run.
