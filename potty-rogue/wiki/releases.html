Release Notes

What changed in each version of Potty Rogue. Numbers and names link into the rest of the wiki so they stay honest.

Version 1.0.14

November 16, 2026

Elite spawns start earlier but stay capped so early and mid floors don't fill with gold rings.

  • Elites can appear from floor 3 (was 4).
  • At most 1 elite per floor through floor 15, 2 through floor 25, then unlimited. Boss floors still never spawn elites.

Version 1.0.13

November 16, 2026

The Compoopdium now unlocks each baddie's elite (+) entry the first time you meet one.

  • Seeing an elite fills in that baddie's Elite card in the Compoopdium (gold ring, extra health, double XP/points, every-other-hit stun). Regular sightings still unlock the base entry only.

Version 1.0.12

November 16, 2026

Utility items no longer spend your turn: heal, buff, and freeze without letting the baddies act.

  • Wet Wipes, Prune Juice, and Ice-Cold Seat no longer spend your turn. Use them freely as long as they have an effect (no wipe at full health; Ice-Cold Seat needs someone freezable).
  • Attacks, moves, Hold It!, Flush Portal, and cabinet opens still spend the turn as before.

Version 1.0.11

November 15, 2026

The first post-launch content drop: more levels, new item and perk toys, deeper late floors, and an XP curve that rewards clearing floors without racing past the boss empty-handed.

  • Level cap raised to 16 (was 11), so a long sitting can pick 15 perks. See Perks.
  • XP retuned: clearing floor 1 is enough to hit level 2; later levels slow from about 1 floor per level early to about 2 by the top. Quick Learner still gets you a couple levels higher by the boss. Full table on Perks.
  • New legendary perk Senna Surge: permanent ×2 damage for the rest of the run, stacking with every other damage boost.
  • Extra Rolls is gone. In its place, item-power perks: Ghost Pepper, Sauce Splash, Jumbo Bath Bomb, Extra Fizzy, High Voltage, and Extra Zap.
  • Prune Juice now triples your next damaging action — plunger, TP, Hot Sauce (and its spill), Air Freshener, Bath Bomb, or Static Bath Mat — and stacks up to 3.
  • Wet Wipes stack up to 10 in one bag slot.
  • New legendary item Moistomatic: keep it in your bag and it produces a Wet Wipe at the start of every floor.
  • New uncommon perk Bidet: Wet Wipes restore 2 hearts instead of 1.
  • Second Wind heals every 2 flushes (was 3).
  • Cabinet Raider doubles cabinet points per stack (×2, then ×4) — 300 points from a floor-20 cabinet at max.
  • Deep floors bulk up: every baddie gains HP from floor 15 on and damage from floor 20 on. Details on Floors.
  • Floaters take one more dead turn before reviving, stop stacking puddles on the same tile, and are soft-capped before floor 15.
  • Baddies can path across floor loot again (they no longer get stuck around dropped items).
