Release Notes

What changed in each Play Store ship of Potty Rogue (Internal or Production). Interim builds between uploads are folded into the next shipped version. Numbers and names link into the rest of the wiki so they stay honest.

Version 1.0.32

November 16, 2026

Snappier combat feel, clearer perk stacking on level-up, and a FLUSHED screen that names the killing blow.

  • Level-up perk cards show the stack you'd get if you pick them again (×2 next to the name, plus "2 of 3" when the perk stacks).
  • Baddie walks snap into place — only hit/attack beats hold the turn. Attack arrows, hit boxes, and damage numbers draw above avatars so they stay readable.
  • No more long deathblow hold. The FLUSHED / Work Log line names who flushed you, how (bite, gaze, Death Sauce, spill, and friends), and how many hearts it cost — e.g. "Flushed by a Hot Sauce Imp+'s Death Sauce for 3 hearts on floor 12".

Version 1.0.28

November 16, 2026

Clearer inspect and rarity UI, Cut Corners S.B.D.+, deeper-floor warnings, and a big polish pass since 1.0.18.

  • Plumber's Guild starters: unlock prices are 10× the old list, then the old prices become the Flush Coin fee charged when you start a run with that starter equipped. Your equipped starter shows on the title screen; the Work Log records the spend.
  • Golden Plunger copy now points you at the Extras menu. Flush Coins stay pinned while you scroll the Guild.
  • Seeing an elite baddie for the first time also unlocks its regular Compoopdium entry. Item pages include perk steppers so you can preview damage and target counts.
  • Tap a baddie on the board to inspect it in the item bar: floor-scaled hit power, current/max HP, and each kind's signature quirks (Euphoria from Laid Brick, Floater puddles, indigestible Sweet Corn, Uh-Oh pops, resting Mystery Burritos, charging Imps, boss rules, and more).
  • Heavy Hitter / Triple-Digit Plunge now count total damage from one action across every baddie hit (Air Freshener, Bath Bomb, Static Bath Mat, and friends), not just one target.
  • Quests you haven't beaten yet are 5× as likely to be picked (was 4×).
  • World Throwing TP Roll pickups give 3 instead of 2.
  • Stink Eyes never face a wall or cabinet with an empty gaze — every facing covers at least one open cell.
  • Drop an item on a Floater puddle to block its revive (noted in Floaters tips). Hot spills hurt baddies that end their turn on them; killing a Floater on a hazard clears that tile.
  • Level-up and Well-Stocked Cabinet pickers ignore taps for half a second so fast runs don't mis-click. Toasts sit at the top of the screen and pause while those overlays are up.
  • Bag slots stay put when you use, drop, or swap; Hot Sauce, Bath Bomb, and Air Freshener stack up to 3; Well-Stocked rolls three distinct offers.
  • Prune Juice is now Common; Ice-Cold Seat is Uncommon. Among commons, Throwing TP Roll weigh 2× other commons (was 4× Hold It! / Wet Wipe).
  • Ghost Pepper still adds +2 Hot Sauce throw damage per stack, and now also +1 spill burn per stack.
  • If your bag is full when a new floor starts, Moistomatic toasts that it couldn't make a Wet Wipe.
  • Baddies that move on the same turn slide one after another.
  • Noisy pickup and floor-start toasts are gone; the stairs lock toast stays.
  • Rarity on Compoopdium cards, bag slots, item info, and choice cards is a colored outline on the cream panel (not a full tint fill).
  • S.B.D.+ elite ability is now Cut Corners: it paths on diagonals, but still only bites a tile directly next to you. Plunger hits work even while it sits in a wall.
  • Arriving on a floor where baddie health or damage steps up (15, 20, 25, …) toasts "The baddies got badder!" with the current bonuses. See Floors.
  • Baddies path around the stairs/drain instead of walking onto it (including S.B.D.s and the Gas Giant).

Version 1.0.18

November 16, 2026

Safer Flush Coin and Guild progress saves on Android, plus clearer wiki notes on which unlocks are permanent.

  • Android now writes your profile to disk (and nudges Auto Backup) whenever you earn Flush Coins or buy a permanent Guild unlock, so a force-stop is less likely to roll those purchases back.
  • Saved profiles get a small upgrade pass on load (classic skin always owned, invalid loadouts cleared) so older or partially-written saves stay valid.
  • Wiki: new Plumber's Guild page lists Flush Coin cosmetics and starting unlocks (One-time pay once, keep forever).

Version 1.0.17

November 16, 2026

Named elite abilities, Euphoria from Laid Brick kills, denser floor loot, free utility items, and Well-Stocked Cabinets.

  • Wet Wipes, Prune Juice, and Ice-Cold Seat no longer spend your turn. Use them freely as long as they have an effect (no wipe at full health; Ice-Cold Seat needs someone freezable). Attacks, moves, Hold It!, Flush Portal, and cabinet opens still spend the turn.
  • The Compoopdium unlocks each baddie's Elite (+) card the first time you meet one (gold ring, extra health, double XP/points). Regular sightings still unlock the base entry only.
  • Elites can appear from floor 3, capped at 1 per floor through floor 15, 2 through floor 25, then unlimited. Boss floors still never spawn elites.
  • Named elite abilities replace the shared every-other-hit stun: Extra Clump, Quick Return, Wandering Gaze, In the Walls, Death Sauce, Hard Landing, Raging Burrito, Stubborn Husk, and Double Toot. Toilet Roach+ stays bulk-only. Sweet Corn+ keeps Stubborn Husk; Mystery Burrito+ (Raging Burrito) walks every turn.
  • Flushing a Laid Brick or Laid Brick+ grants Euphoria: +1 damage on your next plunger hit. Multiple flushes stack. Items don't spend it.
  • Floor items: one 50% roll per two baddies on the floor (was a flat 35% for at most one). Lucky Find now applies to floor drops as well as cabinets.
  • New rare perk Well-Stocked Cabinets: opening a Medicine Cabinet offers 3 random items; pick one to drop there.

Version 1.0.11

November 15, 2026

The first post-launch content drop: more levels, new item and perk toys, deeper late floors, and an XP curve that rewards clearing floors without racing past the boss empty-handed.

  • Level cap raised to 16 (was 11), so a long sitting can pick 15 perks. See Perks.
  • XP retuned: clearing floor 1 is enough to hit level 2; later levels slow from about 1 floor per level early to about 2 by the top. Quick Learner still gets you a couple levels higher by the boss. Full table on Perks.
  • New legendary perk Senna Surge: permanent ×2 damage for the rest of the run, stacking with every other damage boost.
  • Extra Rolls is gone. In its place, item-power perks: Ghost Pepper, Sauce Splash, Jumbo Bath Bomb, Extra Fizzy, High Voltage, and Extra Zap.
  • Prune Juice now triples your next damaging action — plunger, TP, Hot Sauce (and its spill), Air Freshener, Bath Bomb, or Static Bath Mat — and stacks up to 3.
  • Wet Wipes stack up to 10 in one bag slot.
  • New legendary item Moistomatic: keep it in your bag and it produces a Wet Wipe at the start of every floor.
  • New uncommon perk Bidet: Wet Wipes restore 2 hearts instead of 1.
  • Second Wind heals every 2 flushes (was 3).
  • Cabinet Raider doubles cabinet points per stack (×2, then ×4) — 300 points from a floor-20 cabinet at max.
  • Deep floors bulk up: every baddie gains HP from floor 15 on and damage from floor 20 on. Details on Floors.
  • Floaters take one more dead turn before reviving, stop stacking puddles on the same tile, and are soft-capped before floor 15.
  • Baddies can path across floor loot again (they no longer get stuck around dropped items).
