Bosses

Every run's quest ends on floor 14 with one of these six. Bosses can't be stunned or frozen, so they're gear checks: bring damage, hearts, or a plan.

Before any boss

  • Flush the extras first. The boss floor is topped up with Hairballs, Floaters, and S.B.D.s until it's worth a normal floor's XP. Clearing them often lands a level-up right before the fight, and a level-up heals you to full.
  • Drink Prune Juice early. It sits on your plunger until your next hit, so drink it a few tiles away, not while the boss is swinging at you.
  • Count your hits. Bosses have 9–12 health. Every trade costs you hearts, so fewer, bigger hits win.
  • The stairs stay locked until the boss is flushed. After that, the run carries on as deep as you can go.

How to win

  • The fight has two halves. King Clog has 9 health and hits for 2, and when he goes down he splits into 2 Mini Clogs with 3 health each. The quest is done when both Minis are flushed.
  • The Minis arrive dazed and skip their first turn. Unlike the King, they can be stunned, and two is exactly how many baddies you can juggle with plunger stuns. Alternate hits and neither one gets a swing.
  • A Royal Flush doesn't touch the King, but it flushes both Minis instantly and finishes the quest. An Ice-Cold Seat freezes them both. A Bath Bomb hits both at once if they're next to you.
  • Clear the other baddies on the floor first so the Minis are the only things left to juggle.

Best build: Firm Grip ×2 (3 damage) flushes a Mini in one hit and the King in 3. With 3 damage, one swig of Prune Juice (×3 = 9) flushes the King in one hit. Iron Bowels helps you survive the trade. Save a Royal Flush or Bath Bomb for the split.

Boss. 12 health, hits for 2, and can't be stunned or frozen. Long Arm of the Law: hits you from 2 tiles away in a straight line, so close the gap fast or wear it down from range.

How to win

  • Long Arm of the Law: it hits you from 2 tiles away in a straight line. The tile in between has to be open, so a wall, a Medicine Cabinet, or another baddie in the gap blocks it.
  • It can't reach diagonally. Stand diagonal to it as it walks up and it has to step next to you, which uses its turn, and then you swing first.
  • Ranged damage is free damage. If it walks straight at you from 4 tiles away, you get 3 throws before it can reach you: it steps to 3, steps to 2, and only then hits.

Best build: Sharper Rolls is the star here: at 4 throw damage, 3 TP Rolls flush all 12 health before it ever touches you. Extra Rolls keeps the ammo coming. Plunger builds work too: Firm Grip ×3 plus one swig of Prune Juice is 12, a one-hit flush.

Boss. 10 health, hits for 1 twice a turn, and can't be stunned or frozen. Lies still until it sees you down a clear row or column, then takes 2 actions every turn. Line up a big first hit.

How to win

  • It sleeps until it can see you down a clear row or column. Walls and Medicine Cabinets block its view; baddies don't. It always starts out of sight of the spot where you land.
  • Waking up uses one of its 2 actions. Once it's awake, it bites twice a turn for 1 each, and it can't be stunned.
  • Do your prep while it sleeps: drink Prune Juice out of its sight (the multiplier waits for your next plunger hit, no matter how long). Then walk up from a diagonal, step next to it, and hit it hard. You'll take at most one bite while it wakes.

Best build: Big single hits. With Firm Grip ×3 (4 damage), one swig of Prune Juice does 12 and flushes all 10 health in one hit. At 2 damage you need 2 swigs (×9 = 18). Without Prune Juice, every trade costs you 2 hearts, so Iron Bowels and Wet Wipes matter.

Boss. 12 health, hits for 2, and can't be stunned or frozen. Heals 1 every turn you don't hit it, so bring big damage and don't let up.

How to win

  • It heals 1 health on every turn it isn't damaged. Any damage counts: a plunger hit, a TP Roll for 1, a Stink Eye beam, or an Imp's fireball.
  • It starts at full health, so drink your Prune Juice before you start the fight. Drinking in the middle of the fight gives it a free heal.
  • Once you start, keep hitting every turn. If you have to step away, throw something on the way out so it doesn't heal.

Best build: Raw damage. Firm Grip ×3 makes it a 3-hit fight, and with one swig of Prune Juice first it's a single 12-damage hit for all 12 health. Hot Sauce (3 damage) and Sharper Rolls throws keep it from healing while you close in.

Boss. 10 health, hits for 2, and can't be stunned or frozen. Spawns a Floater every 3 turns (up to 3). Its Floaters keep coming back, so go for the boss.

How to win

  • It spawns a Floater every 3 turns, up to 3 in all. Spawning uses its turn, so on those turns it isn't hitting you.
  • Its Floaters come back 3 turns after you flush them, and only the first flush of each is worth XP. Don't farm them; go for the boss.
  • A Royal Flush clears every Floater and puddle on the floor (the Mother Load itself survives). An Ice-Cold Seat freezes the Floaters for 3 turns while you work on the boss.

Best build: Get there fast and hit hard: Firm Grip and Prune Juice end it before the third Floater shows up (4 × 3 = 12 is a one-hit flush of its 10 health). Bath Bomb is great when the Floaters crowd around you.

Boss. 12 health, hits for 1, and can't be stunned or frozen. Counterattack: every plunger hit that doesn't flush it gets 1 heart of gas straight back. It also leaves festering poot on every tile it drifts off of for 5 turns, and stepping in it costs a heart. Drifts through walls, too.

How to win

  • Every plunger hit that doesn't flush it gets 1 heart of gas straight back. Thrown TP Rolls, Hot Sauce, Bath Bombs, and Static Bath Mats don't trigger the counterattack.
  • It leaves festering poot on every tile it drifts off of for 5 turns. Stepping in poot costs 1 heart, so let it come to you instead of chasing it.
  • It drifts through walls, so choke points won't help. Open ground with long straight lanes is your friend.

Best build: Ranged or one-shot. Sharper Rolls ×3 plus Extra Rolls flushes its 12 health in 3 throws with no gas back. Or go all in on the plunger: a hit that flushes it never gets countered, so Firm Grip ×3 and one swig of Prune Juice (12 damage) takes no gas at all.
