The Story

The Kingdom of Porcelain runs on one thing: a steady supply of toilet paper. So when King Clog crept up out of the pipes and made off with the kingdom's Last Roll, things got desperate fast.

The royal plumbers drew straws. The short straw went to Sir Plunge-a-Lot, a knight of the Plumber's Guild with a trusty plunger, a bag with room for 4 things, and an alarming lack of fear. His orders: go down the drain, through every restroom between here and the bottom, and put things right.

It turns out King Clog isn't the only one down there. The Law patrols the stalls, the Drain Snake coils in the pipes, the Hoarder sits on the Golden Roll, the Mother Load keeps churning out Floaters, and the Gas Giant is stinking up the whole Sewers. Every trip down (every sitting) the Guild hands Sir Plunge-a-Lot one of these jobs as his quest.

Along the way he keeps running into a lost Rubber Duck, stuck in a drain and squeaking for help. Nobody knows how it got down there. Bring a Rubber Glove and pull it out for a big reward.

Sir Plunge-a-Lot isn't alone. Dame Plunge-a-Lot and The Royal Flusher can be hired at the Plumber's Guild with Flush Coins, and the Golden Plunger shows up to thank anyone who owns all three Extras.

The quests

Each run picks one of these at random, and quests you haven't completed yet are 4 times as likely to come up, so filling in the Compoopdium doesn't come down to luck. The boss is always on floor 14, deep in the Sewers, and the stairs down stay locked until it's flushed. Finishing the quest is worth 250 points, and the run keeps going afterward for as long as you can survive.

The six restrooms

Every 3 floors the scenery changes, and so does the trouble.

Home Bathroom

Floors 1–3

Where it all starts. Fuzzy bath mats, a shower drain full of Hairballs, and the first Floaters that just won't stay down.

Office Restroom

Floors 4–6

Fluorescent lights, motion-sensor faucets that never sense you, and the first Stink Eyes and S.B.D.s. Elites start showing up here.

Gas Station

Floors 7–9

You had to ask for the key. Hot Sauce Imps, Laid Bricks, and a Mystery Burrito that's been under the heat lamp far too long call this place home.

Stadium Porta-Potty

Floors 10–12

It's halftime and the line is out the door. Sweet Corn and Uh-Ohs join the crowd, and Stink Eyes start looking in more than one direction.

The Sewers

Floors 13–15

Below every restroom in the kingdom. This is where the quest bosses lurk, on floor 14.

The Royal Throne Room

Floors 16+

The deepest, fanciest, most dangerous restroom of all. It never ends, and it doesn't get any easier.

The Compoopdium and the Work Log

Sir Plunge-a-Lot keeps notes. The Compoopdium on the title screen fills in every baddie, item, and perk the first time you run into it. Baddies you've flushed get a green check, and bosses whose quest you've completed get a gold trophy. The Work Log keeps a record of every sitting: how deep you got, what flushed you, and whether the quest got done.
