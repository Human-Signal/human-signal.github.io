The Plumber's Guild

Spend Flush Coins here between runs. Cosmetics and starting items are One-time unlocks — pay once, keep them on your profile. You are not buying a fresh copy every sitting.

At the end of a run, every 25 points becomes a Flush Coin (doubled with the Double Flush Extra). Open the Guild from the title screen.

Starting items

These six are always for sale. Unlocking one lets you choose it as your pre-run loadout. Mid-run bag loot from cabinets and the floor is separate — those copies are only for that sitting.

Wet Wipe

Restore 1 heart without spending your turn. With Bidet, restore 2. Can't use at full health. Stacks up to 10.

Pay once to unlock this as a starting loadout. Each run you may bring one unlocked starter; cabinets and floor drops still give consumable copies. See Items.

Extras (not Flush Coins)

Extra Pocket, Lucky Wipe, and Double Flush are real-money One-time purchases in Extras. The store restores them. Own all three and Golden Plunger unlocks for free.
