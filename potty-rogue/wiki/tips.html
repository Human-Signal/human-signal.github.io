Tips & Tricks

How the game actually works under the lid, and how to use it.

How a turn works

  • You act, then every baddie acts, one after another. Moving, plunging, opening a cabinet, and using an item each cost your action. Bumping a wall doesn't.
  • A baddie that walks next to you has used its action, so it doesn't get to hit you that turn. You get the first swing.
  • If you step next to a baddie, it hits you before you can swing. Never walk up to a baddie; let it walk up to you.
  • Attacks only go straight up, down, left, or right. Diagonal is the safe spot: a baddie on your diagonal has to spend its action stepping next to you.
  • Toilet Roaches and the Drain Snake get 2 actions, so they can walk up and bite in the same turn. Keep that in mind when one is 2 steps away.

Dealing with several baddies

  • Stun juggling. A plunger hit stuns for 2 enemy turns: the rest of this one and all of the next. Hit A, then hit B, then A again, and neither of them ever acts. That works for 2 baddies; a 3rd always gets a swing in. Long Stun adds a turn per stack, enough to juggle 3.
  • Only plunger hits stun. TP Rolls, Hot Sauce, and the other items just do damage.
  • Elites break the juggle. They're only stunned on odd-numbered hits, and only for that turn. Deal with an elite alone, or bring Long Stun.
  • Use the walls. Back into a corridor or a corner so only one baddie can stand next to you at a time. (S.B.D.s and the Gas Giant ignore this.)
  • Friendly fire. Stink Eye beams hit every baddie in the lane, an Imp's fireball hits the first thing it touches, and an Uh-Oh's pop splatters everything around it. Stand so other baddies are in the line of fire.
  • Pick your order. Flush the baddies that hurt most first. Sweet Corn hits for 1 and can be stunned, so it can wait; a Mystery Burrito hits for 3 and can't be stunned, so it can't.
  • Thin the herd from range. A TP Roll on a 1-health baddie on its way over is one less to juggle.
  • Panic buttons. An Ice-Cold Seat freezes everything for 3 turns, a Bath Bomb hits all 8 tiles around you, a Flush Portal gets you out, and a Royal Flush ends the conversation.

Managing your hearts

You start with 3 hearts. Healing is scarce, so know where it comes from:

SourceHeals
Going down the stairs1 heart
Leveling upAll the way to full
Wet Wipe1 heart (can't be used at full)
Iron Bowels+1 max heart and 1 heart now
Second Wind1 heart every 4th flush
  • Level-ups are your big heal. When you're low and close to leveling, flushing one more baddie can be safer than running.
  • Don't rush the stairs at full health. The stairs heal 1; if you're already full, you might as well clear the floor first for XP and loot.
  • Hang on to Wet Wipes until you're 2 hearts down, or for the boss.
  • Level 10 is the cap. After that, level-ups stop healing you, and you're living on stairs, Wet Wipes, and Second Wind.
  • Poot costs hearts. Stepping in the Gas Giant's trail costs 1 heart, and so does each plunger hit that doesn't flush it.
  • So does hot sauce. Hot Sauce and Imp fireballs leave a spill wherever they land, and it doesn't go away until you leave the floor. Stepping in one burns you for 1, so think about where you'll need to walk before you throw. The Imp's spills can flush you; your own will leave you at 1 heart.
  • Mind the pop. Plunging an Uh-Oh next to you costs 1 heart. Throw something at it instead.

Cabinets and loot

  • Cabinets drop Common items 60% of the time, Uncommon 28%, Rare 10%, and Legendary 2%. The only Legendary is the Royal Flush, so any cabinet has a 2% chance of one. The full table is on the Items page.
  • Lucky Find ×2 pushes the Royal Flush to 7.4% and Rare items to 32%.
  • Cabinets block movement and line of sight. They make great cover from Stink Eye beams, Imp fireballs, The Law's reach, and the Drain Snake's eyes. Opening one removes that cover.
  • Cabinet points grow with depth (18 on floor 1, 45 on floor 10, 75 on floor 20), and Cabinet Raider adds 50% per stack.

Builds that work

  • The Plunger. Firm Grip ×3 and a Prune Juice or two. At 4 damage, one swig flushes any boss in one hit. Best against the Drain Snake, the Hoarder, and the Mother Load. Don't waste a swig on Sweet Corn.
  • The Sniper. Sharper Rolls ×3 and Extra Rolls. 4-damage throws from across the room, with no counterattack and no risk. Best against The Law and the Gas Giant.
  • The Tank. Iron Bowels (up to 8 hearts), Second Wind, and Wet Wipes. Slower, but forgiving, and it carries long runs past level 10.
  • The Speedrunner. Quick Learner as early as possible. It's the surest way to hit level 10 before floor 14, which means more perks for the boss.
  • The Jackpot. Double Time. If it's offered, it's almost always the pick.

  • Floaters are only worth points the first time you flush them.
  • The Rubber Duck shows up on every 5th floor until you rescue it (but not on the boss floor). You need a Rubber Glove in your bag when you step on it. That's 500 points, more than the quest itself, so a Rubber Glove from the Guild is a strong starting pick for score runs.

The Plumber's Guild and Extras

  • The Plumber's Guild sells starting items (Wet Wipe, Hold It!, Rubber Glove, Prune Juice, Hot Sauce, Flush Portal) and new heroes for Flush Coins. Pick one starting item before each run.
  • A starting Prune Juice is a great boss opener. Hot Sauce saves your early floors. A Rubber Glove is points in the bank.
  • Extras are permanent one-time perks: Extra Pocket (start with 5 bag slots), Lucky Wipe (one free perk reroll per run), and Double Flush (double Flush Coins). Own all three and the Golden Plunger hero is yours.

The small print

  • Your run saves after every move. Close the app anytime and pick up where you left off.
  • Stink Eyes and Imps can't see through walls or cabinets; the Drain Snake can't either. Baddies don't block a Stink Eye's view, though.
  • Standing on a Floater's puddle holds its countdown until you step off.
  • A Flush Portal can't land on the stairs.
  • Prune Juice stays on your plunger through any number of moves and floors until your next hit.
  • If the boss somehow isn't there to flush, the quest is marked failed and the stairs unlock, so you can keep going.
