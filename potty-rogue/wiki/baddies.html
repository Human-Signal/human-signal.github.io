Elites

From floor 4, any regular baddie can show up as an elite, with a gold ring and a + after its name. Elites have 175% health (rounded), and give double XP and double points. They're also harder to pin down: only every other plunger hit stuns them (the 1st, 3rd, 5th...), and only for the turn you hit them.

Lines up a shot when you share its row or column, then fires a turn later. Move out of the lane! Its fireball leaves a hot spill wherever it lands, for the rest of the floor.

Tip: It telegraphs its shot with an orange lane, so just step out of the row or column. Plunging it cancels the shot.
