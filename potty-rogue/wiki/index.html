The basics in one minute

  • It's turn based. Every time you act, every baddie acts. Nothing moves while you think.
  • Drag to move, swipe into a baddie to plunge it. Your plunger hits for 1 to start and stuns what it hits.
  • Baddies only hit straight up, down, left, or right. A baddie on a diagonal can't touch you.
  • Find the hole in the floor to go down. Each new floor heals 1 heart and is a little nastier than the last.
  • Bump Medicine Cabinets for points and a random item. Items wait in your 4-slot bag until you use them.
  • Flush baddies for XP. Every level-up heals you to full and lets you pick a perk, up to level 10.
  • Every run has a quest. Its boss waits on floor 14, and the stairs stay locked until you flush it.
  • When the run ends, your score becomes Flush Coins (1 per 25 points) to spend at the Plumber's Guild.
