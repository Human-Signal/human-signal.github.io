Items

Items come from Medicine Cabinets, from the floor, and from the Plumber's Guild. They go in your bag (4 slots to start) and nothing happens until you tap Use.

Where items come from

  • Medicine Cabinets. Every floor has 1, and from floor 3 there's a 30% chance of a second. Bump one to open it: you get points and it drops one random item. With Well-Stocked Cabinets, you pick from 3 rolled offers instead.
  • On the floor. For every 2 baddies on the floor, there's a 50% chance of one free item lying around (so 4 baddies → up to 2 items, 12 → up to 6). Deep floors with more baddies can litter more loot.
  • The Plumber's Guild. Six starters are always for sale with Flush Coins (marked One-time on their cards). Pay once to unlock that loadout choice forever; each run you may bring one unlocked starter into your bag. Cabinets and floor drops still give you consumable copies during a sitting.

If your bag is full, stepping on an item asks you to tap one you're carrying. That one drops on your square and the new one takes its place. You can also tap an item and press Drop to leave the whole stack on your square. Either way, step off it and back on to pick it up again.

Drop odds

A cabinet or floor drop first rolls a rarity, then picks among the items of that rarity. Hold It! weighs 3 times as much as the other common items. The Lucky Find perk shifts the odds away from Common for both cabinets and floor items.
