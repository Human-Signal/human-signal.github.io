Items

Items come from Medicine Cabinets, from the floor, and from the Plumber's Guild. They go in your bag (4 slots to start) and nothing happens until you tap Use.

Where items come from

  • Medicine Cabinets. Every floor has 1, and from floor 3 there's a 30% chance of a second. Bump one to open it: you get points and it drops one random item.
  • On the floor. Each floor has a 35% chance of one item just lying around, rolled with the same base odds.
  • The Plumber's Guild. Spend Flush Coins on starting items, then pick one to bring along at the start of each run.

If your bag is full, you can't pick an item up. It stays where it is until you free a slot and come back.
